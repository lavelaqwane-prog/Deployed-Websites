At Gqomfa Funeral Directors, we walk alongside families during their most difficult times with compassion, respect, and dignity.

Rooted in the values of our forefathers, we are committed to preserving cultural traditions while embracing modern funeral practices that bring comfort and ease. Every family we serve is met with care, honesty, and the quiet steadiness that this moment calls for.

Vision

To be a trusted and caring presence in our community, celebrating every life with dignity, compassion, and respect — honouring our cultural heritage while embracing modern funeral solutions that bring comfort, unity, and peace of mind to every family we serve.

Mission

To provide compassionate, professional, and culturally grounded funeral services families can rely on. Through integrity, empathy, and excellence, we walk hand in hand with every family — offering support, comfort, and dignity every step of the way.

What's covered, and when

Cover begins the moment your first premium is paid — but the type of cover determines how soon a claim can be honoured. Here's how it builds over time.

Covered immediately once the first premium is paid and the policy is active.

Day 1
Accidental Death
Covered immediately once the first premium is paid and the policy is active. Includes motor vehicle accidents, workplace accidents, fire-related incidents, and other unforeseen accidental events.
6 mo
Natural Death
A six-month waiting period applies to all natural causes of death. No funeral service is provided for a natural death within this period.
12 mo
Suicide Clause
Covered only after twelve months of continuous, uninterrupted premium payments.
Accidental death cover applies immediately — provided the first monthly premium has been successfully paid and the policy is active at the time of death. Gqomfa Funeral Directors reserves the right to request supporting documents or official reports for accidental death claims.
Policy Premiums

Find your monthly premium

Choose a policy type and an age range to see the monthly premium. A once-off non-refundable joining fee applies on registration for R100.Membership will become active once joining fee and first monthly premium is recieved.

Monthly premium must be paid on or before the agreed payment date each month. Failure to pay may result in suspension or cancellation of cover.

Failure to pay premiums for two consecutive months will result in the automatic policy lapse and forfeiture of all benefits.

Important Notice — A 6-month waiting period applies from the reciept of the first premium. No funeral benefits may be claimed during this period.

What your cover includes

Funeral arrangements and burial services for the main member are conducted directly by Gqomfa Funeral Directors according to the selected policy — no cash payout is made. This applies in every case, including accidental or suicidal death.

  • Pick up (100km Radius)
  • Storage
  • Registration of Death
  • Casket & Cross
  • Wreath
  • Programs(50 color pcs)
